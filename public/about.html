About Us

Insta Downloader is a simple tool that lets you save Instagram Reels, post videos and Stories as MP4 — only for videos you own or have permission to download.

How it works

You paste a link, we look the video up through an authorised API (such as the official Instagram API or a service chosen by the site administrator), show you a preview and stream the file to your device unchanged (original quality). We do not store video files on our servers and never ask for your Instagram password.

Responsible use

Rights in a video remain with its original creator. Please do not re-post other people's content without permission. We are not affiliated with Instagram or Meta. Rights holders can send an objection through the Contact page.
